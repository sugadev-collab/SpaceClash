Other art and technology

Building architecture, tier fittings, troop meshes, cratered asteroid geometry and rock maps, procedural backup skies, planets, UI icons and generated audio were created for this project.

Three.js and addons are MIT licensed. Their license is included in libs/LICENSE.txt.

Optional optimized models

A separate asset pack includes Jungle Jim’s Cool Alien Spaceship, Catfish Mech and the uploaded Sci-Fi Materials GLB, under CC BY 4.0. Textures are resized/recompressed; geometry and available rigs/animations are retained. These are not loaded in the game.
